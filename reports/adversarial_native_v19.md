Issue: The guard projectile launch does not hold the scene mutation slot across the yielding `Launcher.Fire(Self, ShotAmmo)` native call. `ReservePlayerLease` can invalidate the guard while that launch is still pending, and `PreparePlayerLease` sees both `pinBusy` and `sceneBusy` false, restores the scene and returns player-ready before the old native launch has finished. The check after `Fire` only suppresses later script effects; it cannot cancel the already queued launch.
Failure scenario: Start a guard shot, then press E while its `Weapon.Fire` native is pending. In the actual PSC source fixture, reentering `ReservePlayerLease` and `PreparePlayerLease` from the pending Fire boundary produced `(lease=2, ready=True, sceneBusy=False, playerReady=True)` before the old Fire appended its projectile. Thus an NPC launch can occur after the player receives the station and after its pose is reset, potentially shooting in the player's newly selected direction. Existing handoff tests cover pending pin and animation operations but do not cover a pending projectile launch.
Suggested fix: Hold the guard's mutation slot before entering `Launcher.Fire`, and release it only after the call returns, including the invalidated-generation path. Ensure deferred recovery/neutral compensation drains that slot before `PreparePlayerLease` may succeed. Add a source-boundary regression that reserves the station before the old Fire effect completes and asserts preparation remains false until that call has drained; include a load/unload cancellation at the same boundary.
